# Independent speech and visual response candidate

This policy-only candidate changes the observer's decision framing. Speech and a
brief authored visual response are independent choices: a silent visual response
is permitted when a supplied geometric event supports it. Uncertainty about a
person's emotion or intent does not by itself invalidate known geometry. Silence
and retain remain valid for repeated cues, no meaningful change, or uncertain
tracking. No event is assigned a phrase, form, color, or required response.

The hypothesis is that separating these choices may reduce unnecessary retain
choices when speech is unwarranted, while preserving restrained behavior. It has
not been tested with a model and does not establish improved usefulness,
expressiveness, or causal interpretation. Earlier failed or interrupted profiles
remain evidence of their original policy and source.

The mechanical authoring guide, unknown-current-geometry boundary, complete
compose semantics, data projection, tools, ownership, quotas, deadlines, model
configuration and budget are unchanged. No current-geometry descriptor or main
coding context is introduced. Existing provider tests verify the policy hash
against the instructions actually attached to the thread; that establishes policy
attachment, not behavioral quality. A later authorized comparison must report its
own exact policy hash and outcomes without forcing an expression or retrying to
obtain a preferred result.

For an authorized trial, restart Live/client/app-server processes and verify the
policy hash from the actual attached thread instructions; a checkout alone does
not refresh cached policy. Preserve every silent/retain/visual/text/error outcome.
A silent visual response does not establish unsolicited conversational-caption
acceptance; a caption-specific probe can remain unexercised while a separate
visual receipt is valid. No new inference or budget expansion is authorized by
this document.
