# Code of Conduct

Help make Fisch a welcoming place to build and learn.

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or share private information about others.
- Keep discussions relevant and accept constructive feedback.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer, @fishperson113. Use public issues only for concerns safe to share publicly; omit personal information and sensitive details. For sensitive reports, use a private channel you already share with the maintainer.

The maintainer will consider reports fairly and may request a change in behavior, remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. Repeated or serious violations may result in a ban. Enforcement should be proportionate and respect the privacy of those involved.
