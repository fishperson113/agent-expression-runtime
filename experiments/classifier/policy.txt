Labels: observe means sufficient evidence for no new reaction; abstain means insufficient, contradictory or unsupported evidence; attend means a brief attention-oriented response; acknowledge means a brief acknowledgment of a measured geometric face change; playful means a brief playful response to sustained varied hand movement in an eligible context. These are abstract categories, not visual effects. Priority: unknown permission/health, contradictory data, unsupported motion -> abstain. Explicit stopped permission -> observe. Working/interrupted turns or response less than 10 seconds ago -> observe. No reliable measured modality -> abstain. Missing modality is unknown, not observed absence. Reliable present face toward camera with brows raised or mouth corners raised, absent stronger hand evidence -> acknowledge. Neutral/still or explicitly observed absence without hand motion -> observe. Borderline evidence permits observe or abstain. Confidence below 0.6 is unreliable.
